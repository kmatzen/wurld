# WurldCam Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-07_

WurldCam records RGB video, LiDAR depth, per-frame camera poses and confidence
maps using your device's camera and LiDAR sensor.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** WurldCam has no servers, no analytics, no advertising, no crash
reporting SDK and no network code of any kind. There is no account to create.

## Where your captures go

Recordings are written to the app's Documents folder on your device. They stay
there until you delete them. You can copy them out yourself using the share
button in the app or through the Files app, and they are included in your
device backups if you have backups enabled.

If you choose to share a capture, it goes wherever you send it — that transfer
is handled by iOS, not by WurldCam.

## Camera access

WurldCam asks for camera permission because recording requires it. Camera and
depth data are used only to produce the capture file you asked for.

## Children

WurldCam does not collect data from anyone, including children.
